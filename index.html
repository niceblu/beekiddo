🎙️ Natural voice

By default BeeKIDDO picks the most natural voice installed on each device. For one consistent, studio-quality voice on every phone and laptop, switch on the Natural cloud voice (Google Cloud Text-to-Speech). Each word or question is generated once and then cached, so the free monthly allowance is normally more than enough.

0.90–0.95 suits young learners.

How to get the API key (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com, create or choose a project, and enable billing (the free tier covers normal classroom use).
  2. Go to APIs & Services ▸ Library, search “Cloud Text-to-Speech API” and press Enable.
  3. Go to APIs & Services ▸ Credentials ▸ Create credentials ▸ API key, then restrict the key to “Cloud Text-to-Speech API”.
  4. Paste the key above, press Test this voice, then switch the option on and save.

If the cloud voice is ever unavailable, BeeKIDDO automatically falls back to the device voice, so students never get silence.

💛 How buying a grade works

A family registers with a nickname + e-mail (WhatsApp is optional) and picks the grade(s) to buy; the price is shown and calculated for them. You get the order in the Requests tab and in your e-mail inbox, then reply by e-mail or WhatsApp with the payment instructions. When the payment is received and checked, open the grade(s) from the Requests tab — the family is told by e-mail (and you can send a WhatsApp message with one tap). No codes are needed. Locked grades are hidden on the server, so they cannot be opened by tricking the page. Everyone can still try the first chapters for free.

Picking 5 grades never costs more than the all-grades price.
